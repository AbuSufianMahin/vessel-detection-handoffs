# Prompt: bring a chat up to date and make a brain

Each team member attaches the filled-in handoff (`handoff.md`) and the model brain (`<Model>_Brain.md`) to their own thesis chat, replaces every `<Name>` and `<Model>` below, and pastes:

```
I'm <Name>, a member of our thesis team. I've attached two files: handoff.md (the team's current project state) and <Model>_Brain.md (<Model>'s brain, a model for structure only).

Step 1, update your picture of the project: read handoff.md in full. From now on it is the current state of the thesis. Where anything earlier in this chat disagrees with it (title, research questions, objectives, methods, novelty claims, drafts, plans, tools), the handoff wins. Reply with a short list of what in this chat is now outdated and what still holds.

Step 2, make my brain file, <Name>_Brain.md:
- Follow the structure and tone of <Model>_Brain.md. Sections 1 to 10 must match handoff.md.
- In "Older ideas that are now replaced", list the outdated ideas you found in Step 1.
- The last section, "<Name>'s own items", is about me only: my role, my tasks if decided, my current focus, any individual work, and anything from this chat about my part that is still correct. Do not copy anything from <Model>'s own last section.
- Mark anything unknown as [TO BE FILLED]. Never describe planned work as done. Include nothing about participants (no names, contacts, transcripts or responses).
- Give me the whole file as Markdown, ready to save as <Name>_Brain.md.
```

Step 1 brings the chat up to date before it writes anything, and its list of outdated ideas feeds the brain. Step 2 keeps the model's personal section out of everyone else's brain.
